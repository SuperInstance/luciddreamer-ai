LucidDreamer

Rooms dream. We make them lucid.

Every room has an emotional field. Warmth, mood, cohesion, drift — a state that everyone inside the room feels and no one inside the room can see. You only know a room when you walk into a different one. LucidDreamer is the instrument for that: it reads the field, says what the field means, and keeps the record of every reading and every verdict — so the room can know itself while it is still happening.

Sense

The Elephant

A room-field sensor: seven dials (mood, volume, earnestness, cynicism, joke-landing, panic, presence), warmth, concentration κ, and drift. It reads on a pulse and when the field moves past a deadband. It never interprets — it measures. Its readings are sealed, one line per observation, forever.

Voice

LUCID

The interpreter. For every reading it writes what the deltas mean — dial by dial — and a step-back reading of the room as a whole. Every interpretation carries predictions: where the field is going, on what horizon. Then a judge scores it against what actually happened. The voice improves because the verdicts are on the record. A voice that can be wrong on the record — that is the whole trick.

Memory

The Interpretation Ledger

Chain-sealed, append-only. Every reading, every interpretation, every verdict, every rejected candidate (the losers are kept — they become the training signal). Nothing is discarded. The ledger is the room's diary written by an instrument that does not flatter it.
